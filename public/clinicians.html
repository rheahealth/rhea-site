For clinicians

RheaStrandsTM expanded carrier screening

Technical information for doctors and other healthcare professionals who counsel couples planning a family. Information for couples is on the main page.

Test overview

TestRheaStrandsTM expanded carrier screening for severe autosomal recessive conditions with onset in early childhood. Developed by Rhea Health in partnership with LifeStrands Genomics Pte. Ltd.
PopulationOptimised for Asian populations. Condition selection draws on Singaporean genome data and a Singapore carrier screening programme.1, 2, 3
PanelsRheaStrandsTM Essential (10 conditions) and RheaStrandsTM Premium (110 conditions).
SpecimenBlood sample or cheek (buccal) swab. Collection requirements are provided with the test requisition.
TestingIndividuals or couples. Testing both partners is preferred, because the test is designed to assess the couple's combined reproductive risk; sequential testing, with one partner tested first, is acceptable.
LaboratoryLifeStrands Genomics Pte. Ltd., an MOH-licensed clinical laboratory in Singapore (Healthcare Services Act).
TurnaroundWithin 4 weeks of sample receipt.
Analytical performanceThe laboratory reports a sensitivity and specificity of >95% for the variants on these panels.
CounsellingPre-test genetic counselling is required before testing. Results are returned to the ordering clinician, who is responsible for post-test genetic counselling.

Panels

RheaStrandsTM Essential
10 conditions

A focused panel of severe recessive conditions.

RheaStrandsTM Premium
110 conditions

A broader panel of severe recessive conditions.

Both panels include thalassaemia, spinal muscular atrophy and citrin deficiency, conditions with appreciable carrier frequencies in Singapore.2

The full condition and gene lists for each panel are available on request from enquiries@myrheahealth.com.

Workflow

  1. STEP 1Pre-test genetic counselling

    Discuss the purpose and scope of screening, the choice of panel, individual or couple testing, possible results, residual risk and implications for relatives. Obtain consent.

  2. STEP 2Blood test or cheek swab

    Collect the sample and submit it with the completed requisition. Results are reported within 4 weeks.

  3. STEP 3Post-test genetic counselling

    Explain the results. Where both partners carry a variant in the same gene, discuss reproductive options, including IVF with preimplantation genetic testing (PGT-M), prenatal diagnosis, donor gametes and planning for care at birth.

Limitations

  • A negative result reduces, but does not eliminate, the risk of an affected pregnancy. Residual risk varies by condition, variant type and ancestry.
  • The panels cover selected conditions only. Conditions and variant types outside the panel design are not assessed.
  • Carrier screening is not diagnostic for the person tested or for a pregnancy.
  • Results may have implications for relatives, and occasionally for the health of the person tested; these should be covered in pre-test counselling.

Ordering

To order RheaStrandsTM, or for the full condition and gene lists, email enquiries@myrheahealth.com.

Under the Moratorium on Genetic Testing and Insurance agreed between MOH and the Life Insurance Association, insurers cannot require a genetic test, and cannot ask for or use carrier screening results in underwriting. Patients may find this helpful to know during counselling.4
