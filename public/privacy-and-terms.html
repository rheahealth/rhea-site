Privacy Notice

Rhea Health respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data in line with the Singapore Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA).

What we collect

This website does not collect personal data: it has no forms, and it does not use cookies or analytics. If you email us, we collect your email address and anything you include in your message. If you are tested, we may receive your contact details and information relevant to your carrier screening from your healthcare provider and the laboratory.

How we use your data

We use your data to: (a) respond to your enquiry; (b) provide screening-related information; (c) arrange and administer requested services; and (d) conduct service improvements and operations. We will not sell your data.

How your test results are shared

Your results are sent to you and to the healthcare provider who arranged your test. If you are tested as a couple, results are shared between partners only with each person's consent. Your results are not shared with anyone else, including employers, without your written consent.

Under the Moratorium on Genetic Testing and Insurance agreed between MOH and the Life Insurance Association, insurers cannot require you to take a genetic test, and cannot ask for or use carrier screening results when you apply for insurance.

Marketing communications

We only send marketing communications with your consent. You may withdraw consent at any time by following unsubscribe options in our emails or contacting us as described below. We do not send marketing SMS/calls without your express consent and we comply with applicable Do Not Call provisions.

Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated or as required by law, after which we will delete or anonymise it.

Access, correction, and withdrawal

You may request access to or correction of your personal data, or withdraw your consent, by contacting us. We will respond in accordance with the PDPA.

Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our Site does not set cookies, and it does not load content from other websites. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to operate and secure the Site.

We do not currently use advertising, tracking, or analytics cookies. If this changes, we will update this notice.

Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our services, we share data with the following processors under appropriate data processing agreements:

International Data Transfers

Our website infrastructure and cloud services may involve transferring your personal data outside Singapore. Where this occurs, we take steps to ensure an adequate level of data protection is applied, in accordance with the requirements of the Personal Data Protection Act 2012 (PDPA). This includes contractual safeguards with our service providers.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach that is likely to result in significant harm to you, we will:

1. Notify the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) as soon as practicable, and in any event within 3 business days of confirming the breach (for notifiable breaches under the PDPA).

4. Medical Disclaimer

Please read this section carefully.

Rhea Health's carrier screening is a screening service — it is not a diagnostic test and does not constitute medical advice, a medical diagnosis, or a clinical assessment.

A "carrier" result means you carry one variant copy of a gene associated with a particular condition. Most carriers are healthy and do not develop the condition themselves; the clinical significance relates primarily to reproductive planning when both partners carry variants for the same condition.

Limitations of screening:

- Our panels cover selected conditions only, and do not cover all known genetic conditions or all variants within screened genes. A negative result does not eliminate the possibility of being a carrier for conditions not included in the panel, or for variants not detectable by our methodology.
- No screening test achieves 100% sensitivity or specificity. Results should be interpreted in the context of your personal and family history.
- Results are not intended to predict, diagnose, or rule out any medical condition in yourself or a future child.

What to do with your results:

You should discuss your screening report with a qualified healthcare professional — such as your obstetrician, family physician, or a certified genetic counsellor — before making any reproductive or clinical decisions.
